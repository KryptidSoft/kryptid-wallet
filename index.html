Security & Session Management

Choose how you want to close your current wallet session. You can temporarily lock it for a quick return, or completely wipe all sensitive data from this machine.

Option A: Lock Wallet (Create Password for Next Login)

This encrypts your keys into this browser's local storage. On your next visit, you can log in quickly using just your Master Password instead of typing your 12-word seed phrase again.

Option B: Destructive Logout (Wipe RAM & Local Storage)

Emergency termination. This completely erases your private keys from the computer's memory and browser cache. WARNING: You will absolutely need your 12-word seed phrase to access this wallet next time.
